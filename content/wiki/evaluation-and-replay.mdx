Evaluation is not a launch checklist. It is the control system for AI used in operational work.

Replay matters because operational scenarios are messy: missing context, conflicting evidence, ambiguous ownership, sensitive questions, and pressure to act quickly. A good evaluation set includes these realities.

The assistant should be tested for groundedness, refusal quality, escalation judgment, usefulness, latency, and whether it improves the human decision loop.
