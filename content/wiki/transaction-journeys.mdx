Metrics and logs describe fragments of system behavior. Transaction journeys connect those fragments into a path that can be understood by engineering, operations, and business teams.

Transaction Intelligence asks what happened to a customer, process, or business flow across services and time. That makes it a strong foundation for AI-assisted investigation.

When AI can reason over a journey, it can explain impact more clearly than when it only sees isolated telemetry.
