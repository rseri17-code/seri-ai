AI incident systems should not begin with a confident answer. They should begin with evidence, provenance, and explicit uncertainty.

Operational Intelligence depends on a simple discipline: separate observed facts from inferred explanations. A useful assistant can propose hypotheses, but it must show what it used, what it ignored, and why the answer is bounded.

This is especially important in incident investigation. Teams do not need a louder dashboard or a faster hallucination. They need a clear path from signal to timeline to explanation to action.
